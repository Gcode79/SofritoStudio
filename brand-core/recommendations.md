# Recommendations — What to Create Next (Sofrito Studio)

Based on brand core + current pipeline state:

## Priorities (ordered by impact)

### 1. Recipe Video (2-3 clips) — HIGHEST VALUE
**Why:** You have the queue posts ready (Sept 4 demo clip), film videos are the #1 pending user task, and video drives both IG reels + ad retargeting.
**What:** Short (15-30s) recipe clips showing a single step (e.g. sofrito blend, ingredient swap). Use for both organic posts and retargeting audiences.
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 for IG reels / TikTok; 1:1 for feed; 4:5 for carousels.

### 2. Starter Kit Static Ad — FASTEST CONVERSION
**Why:** $9 price point, instant download, upgrade credit creates upsell path. The starter kit is the entry-point product.
**Creative approach:**
- Headline: "The 5 recipes every boricua cook needs" / "Las 5 recetas esenciales"
- Visual: Product cover + ingredient finder screenshot + upgrade credit badge
- CTA: "Instant download — upgrade to any bundle"
**Ratio:** 4:5 (primary), 9:16 (reels adaptation)

### 3. Ingredient-Swap Carousel — DIFFERENTIATOR AD
**Why:** Your unique value prop is mainland ingredient swaps. Most competitors don't address this.
**Carousel (3-5 cards):**
1. "Can't find yuca?" → show mainland swap
2. "No fresh sofrito?" → show frozen/ingredient alternative
3. "Upgrade your starter kit → full bundle" (conversion card)
**Ratio:** 1:1 or 4:5

### 4. Holiday Bundle Push — SEASONAL TIMING
**Why:** Thanksgiving Boricua product exists; seasonal urgency drives conversions.
**Creative:** Warm family imagery + "Tested in the Ortiz kitchen" trust badge + 30-day guarantee.
**Ratio:** 4:5 / 1:1
