## Audience
- **Primary:** Mainland boricua cooks needing ingredient swaps (US-based Puerto Ricans)
- **Secondary:** Heritage/food-curious buyers (non-boricua interested in PR cuisine)
- **Pain:** Finding mainland substitutes for traditional ingredients; maintaining cultural connection to food

## Positioning
- **Voice:** Authentic, family-tested ("Ortiz kitchen"), bilingual, practical
- **Offer:** Essential recipes first → upgrade path (credit toward bundle)
- **Trust:** 30-day guarantee, tested recipes, instant download
