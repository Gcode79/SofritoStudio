## PAGE 2: WHAT IS SOFRITO?

### The Heart of Every Puerto Rican Dish

Sofrito is the flavor foundation of Puerto Rican cuisine. It's the first thing that hits the pan — the aromatic base that transforms rice, beans, meats, and soups from ordinary to extraordinary.

Every Puerto Rican dish starts with the same thing: a good base.

**What's in Traditional Sofrito:**
- Culantro (recao) — the leafy herb that gives sofrito its distinctive flavor
- Ají dulce peppers — sweet, mild, and essential
- Bell peppers — green, for sweetness and color
- Onions — white or yellow
- Garlic — lots of it
- Cilantro — fresh, for brightness
- Olive oil — to bind it all together

### Mainland Ingredient Swaps

Can't find culantro or ají dulce at your local grocery store? No problem.

| Island Ingredient | Mainland Swap | Notes |
|-------------------|---------------|-------|
| Culantro (recao) | Extra cilantro + culantro paste (frozen section) | Find at Latin grocery stores or Amazon |
| Ají dulce | Mini sweet peppers + pinch of cayenne | Closest flavor match |
| Recao leaves | Cilantro stems + leaves | Less intense but works |
| Fresh garlic | Minced garlic in jar | Use 1/2 the amount |

**Where to Find Authentic Ingredients Online:**
1. Amazon — Search "culantro seeds" or "sofrito kit"
2. Boricua Botanica — Ship nationwide, PR ingredients
3. Miami Grocery — Latin specialty items
4. Goya Foods — Recaito jar (backup option)
5. Etsy — Small-batch sofrito makers

## PAGE 3: THE MASTER RECIPE

### Traditional Puerto Rican Sofrito

**Yield:** 2 cups | **Prep:** 10 minutes | **Blend:** 5 minutes | **Cook:** 5 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 bunch culantro (recao) — about 15-20 leaves
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro — stems and leaves
- 1 medium white onion, quartered
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and chopped
- 10 ají dulce peppers (or 6 mini sweet peppers + pinch of cayenne)
- 10 cloves garlic
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt

**Instructions:**

1. **Wash everything thoroughly.** Culantro and cilantro can be sandy — rinse 2-3 times.

2. **Rough chop** the onion, bell pepper, and ají dulce peppers. No need to be precise — it's all going in the blender.

3. **Add to blender** in this order: olive oil, garlic, onion, peppers, culantro, cilantro, salt.

4. **Blend on high** for 2-3 minutes until smooth. Scrape down sides as needed. You want a vibrant green paste with no chunks.

5. **Taste and adjust.** Add more salt if needed. The flavor should be bright, garlicky, and herbaceous.

6. **Transfer to a glass jar** and let cool completely before sealing.

**Chef's Notes from Abuela:**
- "The secret is the culantro — don't skip it. Cilantro alone isn't the same."
- "Blend it smooth. Chunks burn when you sauté."
- "Taste it raw first — that's how you know if it's balanced."

### The 20-Minute Batch Plan

Make a month's worth of sofrito in 20 minutes:

1. **Minute 1-5:** Wash and prep all ingredients
2. **Minute 6-10:** Blend in batches (double the recipe)
3. **Minute 11-15:** Transfer to ice cube trays
4. **Minute 16-20:** Freeze, pop out, store in freezer bags

**Result:** 20-24 frozen sofrito cubes = 1 month of flavor bases

## PAGE 4: STORAGE, TROUBLESHOOTING & USAGE

### Storage Guide

| Method | Container | Duration | Notes |
|--------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Refrigerator | Glass jar, airtight | 5-7 days | Best for fresh use |
| Freezer | Ice cube trays → freezer bags | 6 months | Best for batch prep |
| Freezer | Small glass jars | 3 months | Leave headspace for expansion |

**Pro Tip:** Freeze in ice cube trays first, then transfer cubes to labeled freezer bags. Each cube = approximately 2 tablespoons = 1 batch of rice or beans.

### Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Sofrito tastes bitter
**Solution:** You blended the cilantro stems too long. Next time, add cilantro last and pulse only 3-4 times.

**Problem:** Sofrito is too watery
**Solution:** You used too much onion or didn't strain. Add more culantro and garlic to thicken.

**Problem:** Sofrito turned brown
**Solution:** Oxidation — it's still safe to eat but flavor is diminished. Use within 3 days or freeze immediately.

**Problem:** Can't find ají dulce
**Solution:** Use mini sweet peppers + tiny pinch of cayenne. Not identical, but close enough.

### How to Use Your Sofrito

**Every Puerto Rican Dish Starts Here:**

1. **Arroz con Gandules:** 2 tablespoons sofrito in the oil before adding rice
2. **Habichuelas Guisadas:** 2 tablespoons sofrito sautéed before beans
3. **Pernil:** Rub sofrito under the skin before roasting
4. **Sancocho:** 3 tablespoons sofrito for the soup base
5. **Tostones:** Mix into dipping sauce
6. **Alcapurrias:** Add to the meat filling
7. **Mofongo:** Stir into the garlic butter
8. **Empanadillas:** Use in the meat filling
9. **Asopao:** Base for the rice soup
10. **Anything Puerto Rican:** If in doubt, add sofrito
