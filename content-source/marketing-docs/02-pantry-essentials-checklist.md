## QUICK SUBSTITUTION CHART

| If You Can't Find... | Use This Instead... | Ratio |
|----------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Culantro (recao) | Fresh cilantro + culantro paste | 2x cilantro amount |
| Ají dulce | Mini sweet peppers + pinch cayenne | 1:1 + tiny cayenne |
| Recao leaves | Cilantro stems + leaves | 1.5x amount |
| Green plantains | Yellow plantains (for maduros) | Different dish |
| Pigeon peas | Black beans or kidney beans | 1:1 |
| Salted cod | Fresh cod + extra salt | Soak less time |
| Auyama | Butternut squash | 1:1 |
