If you're not sure where to start, here's the simplest recipe from La Mesa Boricua — you can make it in 30 minutes:

**Café con Leche — Puerto Rican Coffee with Milk**

1. Brew 2 shots of strong coffee (Bustelo or Pilon)
2. Heat 1 cup whole milk until steaming
3. Froth the milk
4. Pour coffee into a cup, add sugar, top with milk
5. Sip. Feel the warmth. Remember.
