## PAGE 1: PERNIL & ARROZ CON GANDULES

### 🎄 Pernil Asado — Perfect Every Time

**The Timeline:**

| When | What to Do |
|------|------------|
| **2 Days Before** | Buy pernil (5-7 lbs). Make sofrito. |
| **1 Day Before** | Season pernil: rub sofrito under skin + all over. Refrigerate uncovered overnight. |
| **Christmas Morning** | Remove pernil from fridge 1 hour before cooking. Preheat oven to 375°F. |
| **4-5 Hours Before Dinner** | Place pernil in roasting pan, fat side up. Roast at 375°F for 4-5 hours (15-20 min/lb). |
| **Last 30 Minutes** | Increase heat to 425°F to crisp the skin (cuerito). |
| **Resting** | Let rest 15-20 minutes before carving. Don't skip this. |

**Temperature Guide:**
- Internal temp: 185-190°F (not 165 — pernil needs to be fall-apart tender)
- If skin isn't crispy: Broil for 3-5 minutes, watching carefully

**Abuela's Secret:**
"The overnight marinade is everything. The sofrito needs time to penetrate. Don't rush it."

### 🍚 Arroz con Gandules — The Perfect Rice

**The Ratio:**
- 2 cups rice : 2.5 cups liquid : 1 can gandules

**The Method:**

| Step | What to Do | Time |
|------|------------|------|
| 1 | Sauté sofrito in oil | 2-3 min |
| 2 | Add tomato paste, cook | 1 min |
| 3 | Add rice, stir to coat | 2 min |
| 4 | Add gandules, sazón, broth | — |
| 5 | Bring to boil, reduce to LOW | — |
| 6 | Cover, cook WITHOUT lifting lid | 25 min |
| 7 | Rest, covered | 10 min |
| 8 | Fluff with fork | — |

**Common Mistakes:**
❌ Lifting the lid (don't do it)
❌ Too much water (rice will be mushy)
❌ Not enough sofrito (bland rice)
❌ Rushing the cook time (undercooked rice)

**Pro Tip:**
"The pegao (crispy bottom) is the best part. Use a caldero and don't stir the bottom layer."

### 🥥 Coquito Quick Reference

**The Ratio:**
- 1 can coconut cream : 1 can condensed milk : 1 can evaporated milk : rum

**The Method:**
1. Blend all ingredients until smooth
2. Chill for at least 4 hours (overnight is best)
3. Shake well before serving
4. Serve cold in small glasses

**Variations:**
- **Classic:** Add 1/2 cup white rum
- **Chocolate:** Add 2 tbsp cocoa powder
- **Spiced:** Add cinnamon + nutmeg
- **Virgin:** Skip the rum — still delicious

**Yield:** About 1 quart (8-10 servings)
