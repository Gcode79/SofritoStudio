## EMAIL 4: NOVEMBER 8 — Pernil Secrets

**Subject:** Pernil secrets from the Ortiz kitchen
**Preview:** The 3 things most people get wrong

---

Pernil is the centerpiece of Nochebuena. It's the dish everyone waits for. And it's the dish most people stress about.

Here are the 3 things most people get wrong:

**Mistake #1: Not marinating long enough.**
The sofrito needs time to penetrate the meat. Overnight is non-negotiable. Season it on Christmas Eve, cook it on Christmas Day.

**Mistake #2: Cooking at the wrong temperature.**
Start at 375°F for 4-5 hours (15-20 min/lb). Then crank to 425°F for the last 30 minutes to crisp the skin. The internal temp should be 185-190°F — not 165°F. Pernil needs to be fall-apart tender.

**Mistake #3: Not resting.**
Let the pernil rest 15-20 minutes before carving. If you cut it too soon, all the juices run out and you're left with dry meat.

**The abuela secret:**
"The overnight marinade is everything. The sofrito needs time to penetrate. Don't rush it."

These are just 3 of the pernil secrets in **Navidad Boricua**. The cookbook has 10 pernil variations, temperature guides, troubleshooting, and side dish pairings.
