## PAGE 1: WHAT IS SAZÓN?

### The Flavor of Puerto Rico in a Packet

Sazón is the seasoning blend that makes Puerto Rican food taste like Puerto Rican food. It's the orange-red color in your rice. It's the savory depth in your beans. It's the flavor you can't quite identify but would miss immediately if it were gone.

Every Puerto Rican dish starts with sazón. It's not optional — it's essential.

### The Two Types of Sazón

**1. Sazón con Culantro y Achiote (with Coriander & Annatto)**
- The most common — the "classic" sazón
- Orange-red color from annatto (achiote)
- Herbal, slightly earthy flavor
- Used in: rice, beans, meats, soups
- Brand: Goya (the one in the orange packet)

**2. Sazón con Culantro (with Coriander only)**
- No annatto — no color change
- More herbal, less earthy
- Used when you don't want the orange color
- Brand: Goya (the one in the green packet)

### Store-Bought vs. Homemade

| Factor | Store-Bought (Goya) | Homemade |
|--------|---------------------|----------|
| **Convenience** | ✅ Open packet, done | ❌ Requires ingredients + prep |
| **Flavor** | Good, consistent | Better, fresher, customizable |
| **Color** | Strong orange from annatto | Can adjust intensity |
| **Additives** | MSG, preservatives | None — just spices |
| **Cost** | ~$0.50/packet | ~$0.10/portion |
| **Shelf Life** | 2+ years | 6 months (airtight) |

**The verdict:** Store-bought is fine for everyday cooking. Homemade is better for special occasions and if you want to control what's in your food.

## PAGE 2: HOMEMADE SAZÓN RECIPES

### Blend 1: Classic Sazón con Culantro y Achiote
*The all-purpose Puerto Rican seasoning*

**Yield:** 12 packets' worth (3 tablespoons)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 tablespoons annatto seeds (achiote)
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon ground coriander

**Instructions:**
1. Toast annatto seeds in a dry pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
2. Grind toasted seeds in a spice grinder or mortar until fine.
3. Combine with all other ingredients.
4. Store in an airtight container.

**Usage:** 1 teaspoon per cup of rice. 2 teaspoons per pound of meat.

---

### Blend 2: Sazón Verde (Green Sazón)
*For when you want flavor without the orange color*

**Yield:** 12 packets' worth (3 tablespoons)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 tablespoons dried culantro (recao) flakes
- 1 tablespoon dried cilantro
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin

**Instructions:**
1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl.
2. Mix well.
3. Store in an airtight container.

**Usage:** Same as classic sazón. Use in dishes where you want the flavor but not the orange color.

---

### Blend 3: Sazón para Carne (Meat Seasoning)
*For pernil, chuletas, and grilled meats*

**Yield:** 4 tablespoons

**Ingredients:**
- 1 tablespoon annatto powder
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne (optional — for heat)

**Instructions:**
1. Combine all ingredients.
2. Store in an airtight container.

**Usage:** Rub 1-2 tablespoons per pound of meat. Let marinate for at least 2 hours (overnight is best).

---

### How to Store Homemade Sazón

| Method | Container | Duration | Notes |
|--------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Room temperature | Airtight jar, dark place | 3 months | Best for frequent use |
| Refrigerator | Airtight jar | 6 months | Longer shelf life |
| Freezer | Small bags or containers | 12 months | For bulk batches |

**Pro Tip:** Label with the date. Sazón loses potency over time — fresher is better.

## PAGE 3: 10 DISHES TO MAKE WITH YOUR SAZÓN

### What to Cook with Homemade Sazón

**1. Arroz con Gandules**
- Add 2 teaspoons sazón to the sofrito base
- Gives the rice its signature orange color
- The #1 use for sazón

**2. Habichuelas Guisadas**
- Stir 1 teaspoon into the bean stew
- Adds depth and color to the sauce
- Makes the beans taste "complete"

**3. Pernil Asado**
- Rub 1 tablespoon per pound under the skin
- Combine with sofrito for the ultimate marinade
- Creates the golden crust

**4. Pollo Guisado (Stewed Chicken)**
- Add 2 teaspoons to the braising liquid
- Seasons the meat and the sauce
- Essential for authentic flavor

**5. Arroz con Pollo**
- 2 teaspoons in the rice base
- Same technique as arroz con gandules
- The chicken absorbs the sazón flavor

**6. Sancocho**
- 2 tablespoons for the whole pot
- Seasons the broth
- Gives the soup its characteristic color

**7. Alcapurrias**
- Mix 1 teaspoon into the meat filling
- Seasons the ground beef
- Authentic street food flavor

**8. Bacalaitos**
- 1/2 teaspoon in the batter
- Adds savory depth to the cod fritters
- Pairs with the salt cod perfectly

**9. Pasteles**
- Mix into the masa (dough)
- Colors and seasons the pasteles
- Essential for holiday pasteles

**10. Asopao de Pollo**
- 2 tablespoons for the rice soup
- Seasons the broth and rice
- Creates the signature orange hue

### The Sazón Principle

In Puerto Rican cooking, sazón isn't just a seasoning — it's a philosophy. It means "to season" or "to flavor." Every dish needs sazón. Every dish needs that layer of flavor that ties everything together.

When you make your own sazón, you're not just saving money or avoiding additives. You're taking control of your flavor. You're continuing a tradition that goes back generations.

That's sazón. That's the Ortiz kitchen. That's Puerto Rican cooking.
