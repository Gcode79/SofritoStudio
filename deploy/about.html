Nuestra historia

Every dish starts with the same thing: a good base.

Illustrative photo (licensed) — the family story lives in the recipes.

Sofrito is that base — culantro, peppers, garlic, and love, sautéed into the flavor of home. And just like the sauce, Sofrito the brand started from one simple truth: the recipes of the Ortiz family kitchen belong to everyone who misses them.

I grew up in the Ortiz kitchen, learning from abuelas who measured with their hands and never wrote anything down. When I moved off the island, the recipes went with me — but only in memory. So I tested, measured, wrote every one of them down, and made each dish so foolproof that anyone can cook boricua from the Ortiz kitchen, anywhere in the world.

Every product here is bilingual, tested in the Ortiz kitchen and refined until it just works. Whether you're feeding three kids on a Tuesday or roasting pernil for Nochebuena, you're not just making food — you're keeping the tradition alive.
