Pernil

My abuela's pernil secret that no cookbook prints

By Marisol T. · August 21, 2026

Every recipe says "rub the marinade, roast overnight." My abuela said the real secret is when you cut the garlic — mash it at the very last minute, not ahead, or it turns bitter. And she never covered the pan. Ever. "El pernil necesita respirar," she'd say. What's the one thing your family insists on that you never see in a recipe?

Mainland Swaps

The one mainland ingredient I had to give up on (and the fix)

By Eddie R. · August 18, 2026

I searched three states for culantro and almost used habanero instead of ají dulce because they looked alike. (Spoiler: do NOT do that.) My fix ended up being a jar of Goya recaito plus fresh cilantro from the corner market. What mainland ingredient still trips you up — and what's your workaround?
