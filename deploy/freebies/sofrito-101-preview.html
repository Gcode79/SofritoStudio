Before you cook

Why sofrito, and the swap sheet

Sofrito is the soul of Puerto Rican cooking. Before the rice hits the pot, sofrito is there — frying gently until the kitchen smells like the island. And yes, you can build it on the mainland.

IngredientIts jobMainland swap
Culantro (recao)The backbone — boricua perfume2 parts cilantro + a little green bell pepper
Ajíes dulcesSweet, fruity, no heatExtra green bell pepper
Green + red bell pepperColor + sweetnessAny sweet pepper
Onion & garlicDepth + punchNever swap these — the law
Olive oilBinder + freezer friendAny neutral oil
Vinegar + saltBrightness + shelf lifeLime juice shortens fridge life
Pro tip: pulse, rest, pulse. Over-blending heats the base and turns it brown; coarse and bright green is the goal.
