Mofongo

Card 1 / 8
Smashed green plantain with garlic — la estrella
Serves: 4Time: 45 minLevel: Medium

Ingredients

  • 4 green plantains
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup chicharrón (optional)
  • ½ cup chicken broth, warm
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Peel & cut plantains into 1½-in rounds. Fry in oil until golden and soft.
  2. Pound garlic + salt into a paste in a pilón or bowl.
  3. Add plantains + chicharrón; pound while drizzling olive oil.
  4. Add warm broth to loosen. Mold into balls or unmold from a bowl.
  5. Drizzle with mojo de ajo. Serve hot.
En español

Fríe los plátanos verdes, machácalos con ajo, chicharrón y aceite; afloja con caldo tibio. Sirve con mojo.

Arroz con Pollo

Card 2 / 8
Yellow chicken & rice — el clásico de los domingos
Serves: 6Time: 45 minLevel: EasySofrito: 1 cube

Ingredients

  • 6 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 sofrito cube
  • 2 tsp sazón
  • 2 cups medium-grain rice
  • 3 cups hot chicken broth
  • ½ cup alcaparrado
  • 2 tbsp olive oil • salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Season chicken with sazón, salt, pepper. Brown 4–5 min per side. Set aside.
  2. Cook the sofrito cube 2 min until fragrant.
  3. Add rice; stir to coat. Add alcaparrado.
  4. Return chicken, pour in hot broth, bring to a boil.
  5. Cover, low heat, 20 min without lifting.
  6. Rest 5 min, fluff, serve.
En español

Dora el pollo con sazón. Sofríe el sofrito, saltea el arroz, vuelve a poner el pollo, añade caldo caliente y tapa a fuego bajo 20 minutos. Reposa 5 y esponja.

Arroz con Gandules

Card 3 / 8
The #1 searched boricua rice — el arroz de fiesta
Serves: 8Time: 45 minLevel: EasySofrito: 1 cube

Ingredients

  • 3 cups medium-grain rice
  • 1 can gandules, liquid reserved
  • 1 sofrito cube
  • 2 tsp sazón
  • 2 tbsp alcaparrado
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Gandules liquid + water to 4½ cups

Steps

  1. Rinse & drain the rice.
  2. Cook the sofrito cube in oil 2 min until fragrant.
  3. Add rice, sazón, alcaparrado; stir 3 min.
  4. Add gandules + liquid to 4½ cups total; salt.
  5. Boil, cover, simmer low 20 min without lifting.
  6. Rest 10 min, fluff, serve.
En español

Sofríe el sofrito, saltea el arroz con sazón, añade los gandules con su jugo y agua hasta 4½ tazas. Tapa y cocina 20 min; reposa 10.

Habichuelas Guisadas

Card 4 / 8
Stewed pink beans — el acompañante eterno
Serves: 6Time: 40 minLevel: EasySofrito: 1 cube

Ingredients

  • 2 cans pink beans (or 1 lb dried, pre-cooked)
  • 1 sofrito cube
  • ½ cup tomato sauce
  • 2 tsp sazón
  • ½ cup calabaza, cubed
  • 2 tbsp alcaparrado
  • 3 cups water or broth • 3 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Cook the sofrito cube in oil 2 min.
  2. Add tomato sauce, sazón, alcaparrado; cook 3 min.
  3. Add beans, calabaza and liquid; simmer 20 min uncovered.
  4. Simmer longer for a thicker sauce; season.
  5. Serve over arroz blanco.
En español

Sofríe el sofrito con salsa de tomate, añade las habichuelas, calabaza y agua. Hierve suave 20–25 min hasta espesar.

Tostones

Card 5 / 8
Twice-fried green plantain — el crujido perfecto
Serves: 4Time: 30 minLevel: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 green plantains
  • 1½ cups canola oil
  • Flaky salt
  • Mojo de ajo to serve

Steps

  1. Peel plantains, cut into 1-in rounds.
  2. Fry at 350°F 3–4 min until pale gold. Drain.
  3. Smash each round to ½ in with a tostonera or plate bottom.
  4. Fry again 2–3 min until golden and crisp.
  5. Salt while hot. Serve with mojo.
En español

Fríe los plátanos verdes, aplástalos y fríe de nuevo hasta dorar. Sal al momento y sirve con mojo.

Amarillos (Maduros)

Card 6 / 8
Sweet ripe plantain — el dulce frito
Serves: 4Time: 15 minLevel: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe plantains, black-spotted
  • ½ cup canola oil
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Peel plantains, slice diagonally into ¾-in pieces.
  2. Fry over medium 3–4 min per side until deep golden.
  3. Drain, sprinkle with salt.
  4. Serve with rice & beans or a fried egg on top.
En español

Fríe el plátano maduro a fuego medio hasta que acaramele. Escurre y sal al momento.

Pernil

Card 7 / 8
Slow-roasted pork shoulder — el plato de Nochebuena
Serves: 12Time: 6 hrs + overnightLevel: Easy, mostly waiting

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 lb) pork shoulder, skin scored
  • 12 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp adobo
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup distilled vinegar

Steps

  1. Pound garlic, adobo, oregano + oil into a paste; add vinegar.
  2. Rub deep into scored skin and all over.
  3. Marinate overnight in the fridge (12–24 hrs).
  4. Roast at 300°F uncovered until fork-tender, 5–6 hrs.
  5. Raise to 450°F for the last 15–20 min to crisp the skin.
  6. Rest 30 min, pull, serve with arroz con gandules.
En español

Adoba el pernil con la pasta de ajo la noche antes. Asa a 300°F por 5–6 horas hasta que se deshaga. Sube el horno para tostar el cuero y descansa 30 min.

Coquito

Card 8 / 8
Puerto Rican eggnog — el brindis de la Navidad
Makes: 6 cupsTime: 15 min + chillLevel: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 can cream of coconut
  • 1 can condensed milk
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1½ cups white rum (optional)

Steps

  1. Blend the four milks with vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg.
  2. Stir in rum to taste.
  3. Chill at least 2 hrs.
  4. Pour, dust with cinnamon, toast the family.
En español

Licúa las cuatro leches con vainilla, canela y nuez moscada. Añade el ron, enfría y sirve espolvoreado con canela.

Sofrito Cheat Sheet

One cube, one dish — always
1 cubeper dish
14 cubesper tray ≈ 14 dinners
3 monthsin the freezer
Master ratios (per ~2 cups)
  • 1 bunch culantro (recao)
  • 15 ajíes dulces
  • 2 green + 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 large onion • 1 head garlic
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar • 1 tbsp salt
The method in 4 moves
  • Rough-chop everything.
  • Pulse to coarse — not a purée.
  • Stir in oil, vinegar, salt; taste.
  • Jar it, or cube it and freeze.
Mainland swaps
  • Culantro → 2 parts cilantro + green bell pepper
  • Ají dulce → extra green bell pepper
  • No annatto? → skip it, sazón adds color
Keep it green
  • Pulse, rest, pulse — heat turns it brown.
  • Oil cap on the jar = 3 weeks in the fridge.
  • Label frozen bags with the date.
  • Bitter? Seed the peppers. Flat? More garlic.
One cube rule: a single frozen cube seasons one pot of rice or guiso. Grab it straight from the freezer into the hot pan — no thawing.
