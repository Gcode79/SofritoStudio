Common questions

When should I start planning my Nochebuena menu?

Start 2-3 weeks before. The cookbook includes a complete timeline from shopping to serving, so you know exactly what to prep when.

Can I make everything ahead?

Yes — most recipes include make-ahead instructions. The pernil marinates overnight, pasteles can be wrapped and frozen, and coquito gets better with time.
