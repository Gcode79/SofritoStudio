# Business Baseline — Sofrito Studio

Status: READ-ONLY assessment (no analytics data available — marked UNKNOWN where applicable)
Brand: Sofrito Studio (sofritostudio.com)
Market: Premium Puerto Rican (Boricua) food/culture ecommerce

---

## Primary Customer
- Mainstream audience: Mainland Puerto Rican cooks seeking authentic recipes with mainland-available ingredients
- Secondary audience: Heritage/food-curious buyers interested in Puerto Rican cuisine
- Pain points: Finding mainland substitutes for traditional ingredients; maintaining cultural connection to food; needing bilingual (EN/ES) content
- Demographic: Likely bilingual, family-centered, values authenticity and tradition

## Primary Product
- Digital product: Sofrito Starter Kit ($9) — 5 core recipes (sofrito, arroz con pollo, pernil, tostones, flan) + ingredient finder + mainland swaps
- Format: Instant-download PDF, bilingual (EN/ES)
- Trust signals: "Tested in the Ortiz kitchen", 30-day guarantee, instant download, upgrade credit toward any bundle

## Secondary Products
- La Mesa Boricua ($47) — 30-recipe bilingual cookbook
- Sofrito Masterclass (educational product)
- Street Food Bundle
- Thanksgiving Boricua (seasonal bundle)
- Various PDF guides (Postres Boricuas, Pernil Playbook, Navidad Boricua, Meal Prep)
- Printables and EPUB versions available

## Offer Hierarchy (Verified)
1. FREE CONTENT (recipe articles, blog posts, social content) — acquisition
2. FREE LEAD MAGNET (Sofrito 101 / Starter Kit download) — email capture
3. LOW-COST PRODUCT ($9 Starter Kit) — first purchase, entry point
4. CORE PRODUCT ($47 Mesa Boricua) — main revenue driver
5. BUNDLE ($67/$97 Kitchen Bundle / Full Table) — upsell, AOV increase
6. PREMIUM / MEMBERSHIP (planned) — recurring revenue
