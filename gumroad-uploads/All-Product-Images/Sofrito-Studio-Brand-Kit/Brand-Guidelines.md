## Logo Variations

### Available Formats
1. **logo.svg** - Primary logo (horizontal)
2. **logo-white.svg** - White version for dark backgrounds
3. **logo-badge.svg** - Badge/icon version
4. **flag.svg** - Puerto Rican flag

### Logo Usage
- Minimum clear space: 24px on all sides
- Minimum size: 32px height
- Never distort or rotate
- Use white version on dark backgrounds

## Imagery Style

## Design Principles

1. **Authentic** - Real family recipes, not generic content
2. **Bilingual** - English and Spanish throughout
3. **Approachable** - No culinary degree required
4. **Tropical** - Puerto Rican colors and vibes
5. **Professional** - Clean, modern design
