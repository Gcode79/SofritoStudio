Sofrito in 10 Minutes

The flavor base of every Puerto Rican dish — batched in minutes, frozen for a month. From the Ortiz kitchen, for your cocina.

Yields: ~3 cupsPrep: 10 minKeeps: 3 months frozen

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches recao (culantro)
  • 2 green cubanelle peppers (or green bell)
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 head garlic, peeled
  • 1/2 bunch cilantro
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt (or to taste)
Mainland swap: can't find recao? Use 1 bunch cilantro + 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley and a pinch more salt — close enough to start.

Steps

  1. Rough chop everything so it fits a blender or food processor.
  2. Blend in batches with the oil and salt until it forms a coarse green paste.
  3. Taste and adjust salt — sofrito should be vibrant, not watery.
  4. Store: fridge for a week, or spoon into an ice-cube tray and freeze for up to 3 months. One cube = one dish.
Use it tonight: a spoonful of sofrito into sautéed onions starts nearly every boricua dish — arroz, beans, chicken, pernil. Start here, taste the difference.
