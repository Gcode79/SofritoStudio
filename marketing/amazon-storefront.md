# Amazon Storefront (Affiliate) — Sofrito Studio

Made with Sazón-style affiliate income: recommend the kitchen tools boricua cooking needs and earn a commission. Runs on any traffic level — a fast, free win alongside product sales.

## 1. Sign up (you, ~10 min)
1. **Amazon Associates**: `affiliate-program.amazon.com` → sign up (Amazon account, website required — use sofritostudio.com)
2. Choose the **Amazon Influencer / Storefront** program (lets you curate a public storefront URL: `amazon.com/shop/sofritostudio`) — approval takes a few days to a few weeks; the classic Associates links work immediately meanwhile.
3. On approval, you get a **tracking ID** (e.g. `sofritostudio-20`) and your storefront link.
4. **Give me your tracking ID** → I wire the affiliate links into the product pages and blog posts (sofrero, caldero, pilon links → `?tag=sofritostudio-20`).
