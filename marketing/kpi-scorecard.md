# KPI Scorecard — Sofrito Studio

Check once a month (fits <5 hrs/wk). Focus on the few numbers that drive the affiliate + products strategy.

## The 5 numbers to watch
| Metric | Where to find it | Target trend |
|---|---|---|
| **Organic sessions/mo** | Cloudflare Analytics (site) + Google Search Console (if added) | Growing month over month |
| **Email list growth/mo** | Buttondown dashboard | Net new subscribers |
| **Email → sale conversion** | Buttondown + Gumroad | Aim ~1–3% of engaged list buys per campaign |
| **Revenue (products)** | Gumroad dashboard — by SKU | Tripwire→bundle attach rate up |
| **Affiliate income** | Amazon Associates reports | Any consistent monthly income |

## Secondary
- **Pinterest**: monthly impressions + outbound clicks (Pinterest Analytics)
- **Top pages**: which 5 pages drive the most sessions (double down on those topics)
- **List size** (absolute) — the compounding asset

## Monthly ritual (~20 min)
1. Copy the 5 numbers into this file (or a sheet)
2. Note 2 "what worked" and 2 "what flopped"
3. Set next month's 1 priority (e.g., "2 more ES posts", "10 pins/wk", "membership launch email")

## Notes
- **Attribution matters less than direction** — you only need to know if you're compounding.
- The email list is the highest-LTV asset: every new subscriber is a future product buyer.
- Don't obsess week 1; wait for a month of data before changing strategy.
