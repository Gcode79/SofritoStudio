# TikTok / Reels Hooks — Sofrito Studio (secondary channel)

Film-ready scripts (~30–45s each). Post 3–5/wk max. Hook in first 2 seconds, end on the recipe page or freebie. Use #puertoricanfood #boricuafood #mofongo #coquito #latinfoodtiktok #nochebuena.

## 1. The "one recipe" hook
**Visual:** slow-mo of mashing mofongo in a pilon.
**Hook:** "There is ONE recipe every boricua table needs — and it's not what you think."
**Body:** "It's mofongo. Green plantains, fried, then pounded with garlic and chicharron. The secret? Work fast while it's hot." (10s of action shots)
**CTA:** "Full recipe on my site — link in bio." → blog/mofongo.html

## 2. The "grandma test" hook
**Visual:** cooking arroz con gandules, cut to a family table.
**Hook:** "My abuela would hit me if I got this wrong — so here's exactly how to nail it."
**Body:** golden rice, sofrito bloom, gandules. "Sofrito first, then the rice. Don't stir once it boils."
**CTA:** free Sofrito 101 guide → freebie form.

## 3. The "nobody tells you" hook
**Visual:** pernil skin crackling under the broiler.
**Hook:** "Nobody tells you the real secret to crispy pernil skin."
**Body:** "Dry skin. Uncover it in the fridge overnight, then roast skin-side up. 185°F internal — not 145."
**CTA:** full pernil method → blog/pernil.html.

## 4. The "holiday countdown" hook (seasonal, big spike)
**Visual:** fast montage — coquito pouring, pasteles, the full Nochebuena table.
**Hook:** "Christmas Eve in a Puerto Rican kitchen looks like this."
**Body:** 15s montage, then: "I built the whole timeline — the cookbook has every step."
**CTA:** Nochebuena guide → products/la-mesa-boricua-sales.html.

## 5. The "ES audience" hook (Spanish — untapped)
**Visual:** warm kitchen, sofrito bubbling.
**Hook:** "Si no haces sofrito en lote, estás cocinando el doble."
**Body:** batch tip in Spanish, 20-min plan.
**CTA:** "Descarga la guía gratis" → freebie.

## Cadence (fits the time budget)
- 3–5/wk, batch-film on one day, post across the week
- Reuse Pinterest images as covers; same clips can become Reels + TikTok
- Pin every TikTok → Pinterest (two channels from one video)
