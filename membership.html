Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There's no commitment or contract. You can cancel anytime and you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

What's in the private group?

The private Facebook group is a space for recipe sharing, cooking challenges, and community support. Members share their cooking wins, ask questions, and connect with fellow boricua home cooks. Josh is active in the group too.

Do I get access to existing content?

Yes. When you join, you get full access to the entire archive of past recipes, meal plans, and resources. Nothing is locked away.

How do live Q&As work?

We host a monthly live Q&A session on Zoom. Bring your cooking questions, get personalized tips from Josh, and connect with other members. Can't make it live? Every session is recorded and available for replay.

Is this different from the cookbooks?

Yes. The cookbooks are one-time purchases with a fixed set of recipes. The membership gives you ongoing, fresh content every month — new recipes, meal plans, live sessions, and community access that grows over time.
