The Digital Guide: brand in three weeks

A free, straight-talk guide for food businesses. No formulas that need a marketing degree — just the taste-first method we use with clients.

Week 1 — The taste test

Define your flavor story in one paragraph, pick your palette by what's on the plate, and name the feeling you want regulars to describe to strangers.

Week 2 — The look

Logo that survives a greasy thumb, type that reads on a phone screen, and photos that make people hungry, not impressed.

Week 3 — The rollout

A checklist to land it everywhere: windows, menus, social banners, delivery apps, and the first 30 posts.

The one-page worksheet

Fill it in over a cafecito. It becomes the brief we use if you bring us in — and it's yours either way.

Sample brief

“You post twice a week. This week the Brazier
is tied to the Sunday special: morcilla jam.
Give me three captions in a warm, direct
tone. Say morcilla, say why it matters, and
never say 'authentic'.”

A 35-second recipe story for Sunday, a “last five at 7pm” nudge for the walk-ins, and one post about why the jam is made in-house — all in a voice that doesn't read like a template.
