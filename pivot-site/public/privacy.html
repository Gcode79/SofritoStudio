Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the fields you fill in: name, email, phone, business name, business type, package interest, budget, and message. When you subscribe to the Journal, we collect your email. Page views are tracked only after you accept the consent banner.

What we use it for

To reply to you, quote a package, and — if you opt in — send the drip emails about brand storytelling. We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers.

Where it lives

Leads and newsletter subscribers are stored in Cloudflare D1 and KV, and transactional email is delivered through Resend (hello@sofritostudio.com). Data is retained while it's useful for running the business, and you can ask us to delete it anytime.

Your rights

Write to hello@sofritostudio.com to request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data. We'll handle it within a few days.

Cookies

We don't set marketing cookies. The site stores two local preferences in your browser: your language choice and your consent choice. Nothing is shared off-device without your consent.
