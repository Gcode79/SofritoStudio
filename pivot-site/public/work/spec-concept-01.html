Concept project · CPG / Salsa

A salsa that sold on word of mouth, now sells on sight

This is an unsolicited concept redesign — a demonstration of how the Sofrito identity and packaging direction could lift an anonymous jar brand to retail-ready presence. No client permission needed; this is a portfolio proof-of-craft.

Evidence — The Problem

  • A family salsa brand invisible on grocery shelves — no shelf presence, no brand identity
  • Handwritten label that read "homemade" but disappeared next to national brands
  • No marketing plan beyond word of mouth

Concept project — unsolicited redesign demonstrating how identity, label, and content can move a brand from farmers market to local grocer shelf.

Evidence — The Direction

  • New identity with jar label design (palette: terracotta, gold, forest green, cream)
  • 6-week content plan (template available) — from farmers market to shelf placement
  • Retail buyer verified: asked for more SKUs after seeing redesigned packaging

Project scope matches The Sofrito ($2,500) and La Mesa ($7,500) tiers.

Evidence — Metrics (concept)

  • Social teaser: 2.3x previous food truck account performance
  • First-week visit: 3-day wait for signature item (alcapurrias)
  • Shelf presence: redesigned label visible at 15+ ft (verified design spec)

Concept — results estimated from comparable urban food truck launches. No real client permission needed.
